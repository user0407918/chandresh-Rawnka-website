Investor Charter

Investors should receive fair, transparent and timely information and should be treated with due care.

Investor responsibilities

  • Provide accurate and complete information and keep KYC details updated.
  • Read scheme and product documents before investing.
  • Understand the risks, costs and applicable terms.
  • Use official transaction and communication channels wherever applicable.
  • Raise concerns through the appropriate grievance mechanism when required.
